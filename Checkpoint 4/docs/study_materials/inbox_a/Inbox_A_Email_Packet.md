The signature is part of the scenario. Sender 1–4 track the actual study accounts used for delivery. Keep the A01–A15 labels in the facilitator copy.

Verify that A11 and A12 are skipped and that nonfinancial messages from those same senders remain eligible. Check the scan activity or available processing record. An empty task list alone cannot distinguish an exclusion from a missed extraction.
- Keep these settings fixed during scored activities. Let participants experiment with settings after scoring if they choose.
- Confirm the starting scan has finished before timing the InboxToTask round. Save the initial list before sending the deadline reply. After each session, remove the newly sent replies as part of the reset.
- Match Inbox B with #63 before freezing. Check the same hard cases, a task count within one, and total word count within 20%. Use the same capture scope in Gmail and InboxToTask. Tell participants in both rounds to leave financial mail out of their study list.
