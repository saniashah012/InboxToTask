Prompt

Act as a UX storyboard designer and comic illustrator. Create a 5-panel storyboard that demonstrates a realistic use case for our AI-powered email-to-action system, InboxToTask.

The storyboard should follow one continuous story about a college student who receives many emails and accidentally overlooks an important assignment deadline. InboxToTask detects the deadline in the overlooked email and helps the student take action before it is too late.

Panel 1 — Email Overload

Show a college student studying in a university library while receiving many emails, including campus announcements, newsletters, club messages, Canvas notifications, and other routine updates.

Among these messages is an important email from a professor stating that Assignment 3 is due tonight at 11:59 PM, but the student does not notice it because it is surrounded by many other emails.

The student thinks: “So many emails... I’ll check the important ones later.”

Panel 2 — The Missed Deadline Risk

Later that evening, around 8:15 PM, show the student relaxing and believing that everything for the day has been completed.

Meanwhile, InboxToTask automatically analyzes the incoming emails in the background. Show the system identifying commitments, extracting key information, and recognizing that the professor’s email contains an urgent action:

Assignment 3 — Due Tonight

Emphasize the contrast between the student being unaware of the deadline and InboxToTask recognizing the risk.

Panel 3 — InboxToTask Catches It

Show InboxToTask notifying the student:

“1 Urgent Action Found”

Display a clear task card containing:

Assignment 3
Due: Tonight, 11:59 PM
Source: Email from Professor

Include a short excerpt from the original email as supporting evidence.

Provide three buttons:

View Email | Edit | Confirm Task

Show the surprised student thinking:

“Oh wow! I almost missed this!”

Emphasize that InboxToTask identifies the action, but the student remains in control and confirms it before anything is added to external tools.

Panel 4 — From Email to Action

After the student confirms the task, show InboxToTask converting the overlooked email into an actionable plan.

Display:

Task Added Successfully

Assignment 3 — Due Tonight, 11:59 PM

Show that the deadline has been added to the student’s calendar and task list.

The student immediately begins working on the assignment. Include a simple checklist such as:

Read instructions
Work on Assignment 3
Review and submit

Also show the remaining time before the deadline to communicate urgency.

Panel 5 — Crisis Averted

Show the student successfully submitting Assignment 3 before the deadline and looking relieved.

The course website should display:

Submitted ✓

InboxToTask should also display:

Assignment 3 — Completed ✓

Preserve a link to the original email so the student can verify the source if necessary.

End with the message:

“InboxToTask turned an overlooked email into an action before it became a missed deadline.”

Visual Style

Use a warm, contemporary comic-book / illustrated UX storyboard style with soft colors and expressive characters. Use university environments such as a campus library and student apartment rather than a corporate office.

Keep the same student visually consistent across all five panels. Make the InboxToTask interfaces clean, modern, and easy to understand.

The storyboard should tell a specific user story rather than explaining the technical architecture of the AI system. Avoid flowchart diagrams, corporate-office imagery, huge unread-email counters, dual-monitor “Inbox Zero” scenes, or other imagery that makes the storyboard resemble a generic system workflow diagram.

The overall narrative should be:

Email overload → important deadline overlooked → InboxToTask detects the commitment → student reviews and confirms → task becomes actionable → assignment submitted on time.
